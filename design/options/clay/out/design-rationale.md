## Color
- **Primary #6E5648** — the signature. Chosen to carry the "קליניקה טיפולית — חימר רך, אקסנט קורל" feel; expanded to a full 50–950 ramp so surfaces, borders and hovers all come from one hue.
- **Secondary #DCC9BA** — a supporting hue for depth (backgrounds, alt sections) without competing with primary.
- **Accent #B4543C** — the sparing pop for CTAs and highlights; kept high-chroma and reserved.
- **Neutrals** — bg #FDFCFA, surface #F2ECE6, ink #2B2420, muted #75675D. Light base with a soft tinted surface.
- **Contrast** — every text/background pair was WCAG-checked; the tightest is *best text on accent (white)* at 4.91:1. Primary/accent were auto-darkened if white button text would have failed AA.
