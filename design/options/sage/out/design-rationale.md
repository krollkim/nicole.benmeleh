## Color
- **Primary #4A6B55** — the signature. Chosen to carry the "קליניקה טיפולית — מרווה רגועה, אקסנט קורל חם" feel; expanded to a full 50–950 ramp so surfaces, borders and hovers all come from one hue.
- **Secondary #C3D8C7** — a supporting hue for depth (backgrounds, alt sections) without competing with primary.
- **Accent #B4543C** — the sparing pop for CTAs and highlights; kept high-chroma and reserved.
- **Neutrals** — bg #FCFCFA, surface #EFF3EE, ink #26302A, muted #66756B. Light base with a soft tinted surface.
- **Contrast** — every text/background pair was WCAG-checked; the tightest is *muted on bg* at 4.73:1. Primary/accent were auto-darkened if white button text would have failed AA.
