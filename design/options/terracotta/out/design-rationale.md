## Color
- **Primary #8A4B38** — the signature. Chosen to carry the "קליניקה טיפולית — טרקוטה אדמתית, אקסנט דבש" feel; expanded to a full 50–950 ramp so surfaces, borders and hovers all come from one hue.
- **Secondary #E8CDBE** — a supporting hue for depth (backgrounds, alt sections) without competing with primary.
- **Accent #9A6B12** — the sparing pop for CTAs and highlights; kept high-chroma and reserved.
- **Neutrals** — bg #FDFBF9, surface #F5EBE4, ink #2E241F, muted #7A6559. Light base with a soft tinted surface.
- **Contrast** — every text/background pair was WCAG-checked; the tightest is *best text on accent (white)* at 4.68:1. Primary/accent were auto-darkened if white button text would have failed AA.
