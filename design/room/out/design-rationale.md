## Color
- **Primary #6B5B95** — the signature. Chosen to carry the "הדף הוא החדר. קרקע ניטרלית־חמה שנמדדה מתצלומי הקליניקה, לוונדר עמוק כאות ולא כרקע." feel; expanded to a full 50–950 ramp so surfaces, borders and hovers all come from one hue.
- **Secondary #CDB18D** — a supporting hue for depth (backgrounds, alt sections) without competing with primary.
- **Accent #6B5B95** — the sparing pop for CTAs and highlights; kept high-chroma and reserved.
- **Neutrals** — bg #E2E4E2, surface #CCC9BE, ink #201B1B, muted #6F5E52. Light base with a soft tinted surface.
- **Contrast** — every text/background pair was WCAG-checked; the tightest is *primary on bg (text/icon)* at 4.62:1. Primary/accent were auto-darkened if white button text would have failed AA.
